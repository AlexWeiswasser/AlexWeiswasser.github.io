Fish Party is FunRug Studio's second game, slated to release in March. It's a large capacity, single button, local multiplayer game, with plans of online play as well. Our first game, MAGE, had a long development and learning process, and in comparison, Fish Party is a much more focused, better executed project.

Twelve separate inputs, bots included

Each player gets one button. That button is how you move, how you dash, how you ready up, and change your name. An input system was required that could work for up to 12 players, be effecient, and allow for the addition of bots, and eventual online players. I decided to decouple the player input from the overall list of all fish, allowing bots and online players to own the same type of slot, and not mess with local input managment.

PlayerHandler.cs

In the playerHandler, each local player creates a dictionary key on creation, comprised of their device ID and control path. This allows lookup times for all local inputs to be O(1), making sure that even with the maximum 12 players, effeciency is guarenteed.
